The four verdict labels
No percentage, no bar. A bar implies something is missing from a hundred; these state what was found.
Clear direction
One option is well ahead of the other. Model confidence 80 and above.
Strong case
A solid recommendation with real reasons behind it. 65 to 79.
Slight edge
The options are close. This one is ahead, but not by much. 50 to 64.
Genuinely close
Too near to separate on the merits. Either is defensible. Below 50.
The grey values are shown here for you only — the user never sees a number. "Genuinely close" is the one that earns the most trust: an advisor that admits a tie is one you believe when it does not.
